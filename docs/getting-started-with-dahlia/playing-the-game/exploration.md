# /explore

Eldelvain is made of places, from **Homestead Ruins** where the first portal opened to **Dahlia's Core** at the heart of the project. Each place has its own monsters, and new places open up as you level.

### The Map

`/explore map` shows where you are, every place you can travel to, and the level each locked place opens at. Deeper places pay more for the monsters you defeat there.

### Travelling

`/explore travel to:<place>` takes you to any place you have unlocked. The first time you reach a new place you get a **discovery bonus** of IURA. From then on, the monsters you fight with `/attack` come from where you are, with that place's extra rewards. Monsters are still matched to your level, so a deeper place isn't harder, it just pays more.

If you never travel, `/attack` keeps choosing monsters from all over Eldelvain.

### Searching

`/explore search` looks around where you are, once every 30 minutes. You might find:

* an **item** from the monsters that live there,
* a pouch of **IURA**,
* a piece of the **story**,
* an **ambush**: a monster from the area attacks, and you fight it like an `/attack`,
* or nothing at all.

Searching also counts toward the daily quest **Search 3 times with /explore**.
