# 🎨 For Designers

**Megura** is an inclusive community that welcomes artists of all kinds. From writers to illustrators, musicians to game developers, anyone can contribute and promote their work within the game. As part of this commitment to creativity, Megura will be providing media kits to all artists.

Artists can use these media kits to showcase their work within the game, whether through illustrations, music tracks or in-game content such as quests, monsters and storylines.

Megura believes that creativity and collaboration are essential for a vibrant and engaging game. By giving artists the tools and a place to share their work, Megura is helping to grow a community of creators, developers and gamers. Whether you're an established artist or just starting out, you're welcome to join and contribute your talents to the game!
