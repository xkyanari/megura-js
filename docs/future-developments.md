# 🚀 Future Developments

### Bosses and raids

Tougher bosses that players fight turn by turn, on their own or together with the whole server. Reading the boss and picking the right counter matters more than raw stats.

### Seasonal events

Events throughout the year with limited-time items such as weapons, outfits and other rewards that can only be earned while the event runs. Events will offer a variety of challenges for players of every level.

### A game beyond Discord

In the future, Megura plans to bring Messinia Graciene to a downloadable game for mobile, PC and console, built on the same world and story.

### Megura is more than a project

Megura is a brand that will keep expanding. **Project DAHLIA** is the first part of this franchise, and Megura aims to add more to the Messinia Graciene universe in the future, with even more engaging and immersive experiences.
