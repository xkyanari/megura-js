# 🛡 Our Mantra

### Accessibility

**Megura** aims to be easy to pick up. Everything happens inside Discord through slash commands and buttons, with no accounts or downloads to set up. Guides and in-game hints help new voyagers get started.

### Fairness

Progress comes from playing. Levels, gear and IURA are earned in the game, and the game is balanced so that a player who shows up regularly can keep up, whatever their play style.

### Story First

The game exists to tell the story of Messinia Graciene. Features are built to serve the world of Eldelvain: its places, its factions and its characters.

### Privacy and Security

Dahlia only reacts to explicit interactions such as slash commands, buttons, menus and forms. She doesn't read normal conversations, and she stores only what the game and server features need. See [Privacy and Data](../privacy-and-data.md).
